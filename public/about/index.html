About

I don’t have a perfectly linear career, and I’m not going to manufacture one for a website.

I’m a New York actor, comedian, creator, and producer. The easiest way to explain the mix is that I like being inside the thing rather than commenting on it from a distance.

I act on sets. I perform live. I work on theatre productions. I make social content for brands and the shows I’m involved with. I talk to other people working in entertainment. On my own channels, I document the things I’m trying to figure out in real life.

The site is built the same way: not as four disconnected résumés, but as one place where the different work can make sense together.

Looks vs. identity

The homepage should look like me now.
